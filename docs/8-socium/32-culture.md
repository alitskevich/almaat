# Culture

## Collective Mind (Mentality)

The shared archetypes, symbols, and active ideas that form a culture's cognitive foundation.

It determines what thoughts are possible — invisible to those inside it.

### Idemons (Ideas)

Self-replicating ideas colonize minds. They spread through language, ritual, and media by offering:

- Simple answers IS Replace complex thinking.
- Ready purpose IS Substitute personal meaning.
- No responsibility IS Remove burden of self-direction.
- Group identity IS Inflate sense of significance.

A mind without its own truth becomes a host for external ideas.

### Crowd

When individuals dissolve into a collective, rational deliberation stops. The crowd acts as one organism toward external targets. Neurochemical bonding reinforces in-group solidarity and out-group aggression. Humanity's capacity for rapid collective coordination was an evolutionary advantage — but crowd intelligence is sub-individual, not super-individual.

## Cultural Transmission

- **Heritage** IS Fixed deposits: language, myth, art, customs, institutions.
- **Culture** IS The living process of creating, transmitting, and consuming Heritage.
- **Education** IS Direct transfer of existing knowledge to new carriers.
- **Propaganda** IS Systematic insertion of ideas into mass consciousness through repetition and emotional triggers.
- **Religion** IS Comprehensive idea packages that satisfy existential needs and direct collective behavior.

## Extraction

Society does not only transmit culture — it **extracts** from its carriers.

Wherever the [Animal](../4-human/28-animal.md) has a vulnerability, a market forms to monetize it.

The product is engineered to capture, not to serve: your attention, reward circuitry, status-anxiety, and appetite are the raw material.

- **Attention** IS Hours and focus, resold to advertisers. Feeds, autoplay, and notifications are tuned to defeat your stopping point.

*NOTE*: When the product is free, you are the product.

- **Dopamine** IS The reward circuit, hooked by variable payoff — games, scrolling, betting. See [Conditioning](../8-sustain/05-security.md#conditioning).

*NOTE*: Designed compulsion sold as entertainment.

- **Status** IS Manufactured insecurity, resold as goods that signal worth: cars, clothes, weddings.

*NOTE*: The need is created, then the cure is priced.

- **Appetite** IS Cheap, engineered food tuned past the body's satiety signal. See [Vitality](../8-sustain/01-vitality.md#6-nutrition).

*NOTE*: Optimized for sales, not for health.

**Optimized for the next click, not your good.** An extractive feed does not show what you like — it shows what keeps you going. Because [novelty renews arousal](../4-human/28-animal.md#reward-and-compulsion) (the Coolidge effect), a system that maximizes engagement walks users up an **escalation ladder** toward stronger, more extreme content. The youngest are the richest target — capture during a developing brain's formative years lasts longest. What looks like free choice is the output of massive A/B testing run against your weak points. The subscription is a sideline; the product sold on is your behavioral profile.

**The insider tell.** The clearest sign that a product is extractive is that the people who build it withhold it from their own children — engineers who ration their kids' screens, sellers who avoid their own supply. What is kept from insiders but pushed onto the public is rarely built for the public's benefit.

The defense mirrors the one against [manipulation](../8-sustain/05-security.md#manipulation--defense): make consumption **deliberate, not reflexive**. An appetite you did not choose is one someone else is steering.
