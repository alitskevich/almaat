# The Given and the Constructed

![The Given and the Constructed](/images/1-reality/27-given.svg)

Start from what is actually given: the perceived world is small and the universe is inferred, so the governing question is not what exists but what matters.

An infant is simply shown the world; only later does the `Mind` name it "universe" and bind it into a picture. Your adult perception is that same given, now labeled — you see no more than the infant, you only know more.

## What Is Given

- **The world you perceive is small; the universe is inferred.** What is directly given is a patch of space a few hundred meters across, bounded by your own life. The 93-billion-light-year cosmos, the deep past, the far future — none of it is perceived. It is a construct assembled from science, testimony, and habit: reliable-looking, yet unperceived.
- **Its age is your age — or a few hours.** The manifest world appeared when you became self-aware and ends when you depart; strictly it is remade each morning, since sleep is a nightly gulf in which nothing is perceived and no time is felt.
- **Causality is imposed, not found.** Direct experience contains only phenomena; the causal links that let us infer a vast external world are added by the `Mind` (Hume). The mind-independent, billion-year universe is therefore a mental construct — and consensus is not truth.
- **The evidence of the past is on the screen.** Chronicles, fossils, and artifacts do not sit *beside* you as independent proof; you perceive them *now*, as part of the present given — like a filmgoer who mistakes notes painted into the film for notes left on the seat. There is one viewer: you.

## Old Ground

Berkeley's *esse est percipi* — to be is to be perceived; Schopenhauer's "the present is the only form of actual existence"; Augustine's three times that exist only in the soul (memory, attention, expectation); Husserl's *epoché*, the deliberate suspension of inherited cosmology and "common sense" so the given can be seen plainly.

## The Manifest and the Existent

Sharpen the terms: the "I" *exists but is not manifest* — you never find it as an object in experience, yet nothing appears without it; the world *is manifest but, without an "I", does not exist* — appearance requires someone to whom it appears. This is not solipsism: the manifest is `Being` disclosing itself, and it is shared and mutually agreed across subjects. Behind the many `Agent`s stands a single source — many mirrors reflecting one light — the same claim as [one `Consciousness`, many dissociations](24-transcendence.md#one-consciousness).

## The Redirection

If the far cosmos and deep past are optional constructions that do not touch how you live, the governing question is not *what exists* or *what is fundamental* but **what matters** — what you are, and why life is given. Improving the outer world never improves the state of the "I"; changing the "I" reorders every value. Turn inward: know thyself.
