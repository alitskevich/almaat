# Being

![Being](/images/1-reality/22-being.svg)

`Being` as three sides — perceptive `Sensation`, creative `Consciousness`, and representative `Reality` — and why `Reality` is a readout rather than the source.

## The Three Sides

**`Being`** := the transcendent process by which the whole experiences, invents, and instantiates itself from many points at once.

`Being` is considered as comprised of three sides:

- perceptive `Sensation` experience,
- transformative creative `Consciousness`,
- and representative material `Reality`.

## Reality

**`Reality`** := a `System` used by `Consciousness` to represent perceived `Being` as a whole and in each of its parts — intended to be logically consistent, and to provide explanatory and predictive functions.

*NOTE*: `Reality` is the readout, not the source.

`Reality` is a representation. With no instrument present — no living being observing — there is no representation, yet the thing represented remains.

Solidity, weight, color, sound — every property of the "physical" world reaches us only through sensation, and sensation occurs within experience.
