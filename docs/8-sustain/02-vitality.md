# Vitality

![Vitality](/images/8-sustain/02-vitality.svg)

The physical foundation of human life, health, and performance: the body's ability to function optimally, resist disease, and recover from stress.

## Vital Mindset

- Accept yourself as you are. It is the starting point, not the endpoint.
- Be content with what you have. Know your own limits — they differ from others'.
- Choose wisdom over comfort. Short-term pain for long-term gain.
- Know your purpose. People without a "why" die younger.
- Health is a tool, not a goal. Obsessing over health is its own disease.
- Health requires daily effort. Small habits, done consistently, compound into vitality; panic and "magic pills" do not. Trust time-tested remedies before quick fixes.
- Fear doesn't change behavior. `Discipline` does.
- Help others. Meaningful contribution extends life.
- Keep your mind active and stay curious — learn, solve, create. People who keep learning after 40 preserve their brain's grey matter volume. You age the day you stop being curious about yourself, not the day wrinkles appear.
- Stop blaming — yourself or others. Mistakes are human.
- Use only what you need. Avoid excess.
- Avoid shallow distractions and protect yourself from what overwhelms you. Depth over noise.
- Do less. Most things don't work.

## Hygiene

- Keep your body and hair clean. Wash hands before eating and after the bathroom.
- Water pick, floss, brush, and tongue scrape morning and night. Checkup twice a year.
- Protect your hearing.
- Design and keep your own style in clothes.

## Household Environment

- Shoes off at the door.
- Avoid plastic where you can.
- Circulate air in your rooms.
- Avoid long-distance travel where you can.

## Connection and Focus

- See at least one friend a week.
- Turn notifications off by default and grant them back by name — see [Attention → The Shape of the Day](../7-mastery/07-attention.md#the-shape-of-the-day).
- Limit social media use.
- Never text while driving.

## Longevity

- The older you get, the more effort health requires.
- Get regular checkups. Catch problems early.
- Most chronic diseases come from laziness, excess, and short-sightedness.
- Medicine treats. Only you can prevent.
- Real detox isn't a smoothie — it's sweat. Skin removes over 30% of the heavy metals the liver can't keep up with. Finnish studies link regular sauna use to an extra ~8 years of life.
