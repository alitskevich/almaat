
## VITALITY

**Definition**: The physical foundation of human life, health, and performance,
encompassing the body's ability to function optimally, resist disease, and recover from stress.

### 1. Vital Mindset

- Accept yourself as you are. It's the starting point, not the endpoint.
- Be content with what you have. Know your own limits — they differ from others'.
- Choose wisdom over comfort. Short-term pain for long-term gain.
- Know your purpose. People without a "why" die younger.
- Health is a tool, not a goal. Obsessing over health is its own disease.
- Health requires daily effort. Small habits, done consistently, compound into vitality.
- Fear doesn't change behavior. Discipline does.
- Help others. Meaningful contribution extends life.
- Trust time-tested remedies before quick fixes.
- Keep your mind active. Learn, solve, create.
- Stop blaming — yourself or others. Mistakes are human.
- Use only what you need. Avoid excess.
- Panic and "magic pills" don't work. Steady habits do.
- Avoid shallow distractions. Depth over noise.
- Protect yourself from things that overwhelm you.

### 2. Sleep — 75% of health

- Sleep is the single most important health habit.
- Sleep as much as YOU need. Go to bed at the same time every night.
- 4-7-8 breathing helps you fall asleep: inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8.
- Dark, cool (15–19°C), quiet room.
- Warm bath 1–2 hours before bed helps.
- Avoid bright/blue light for 1–3 hours before sleep.
- Sleep quality depends on: light, temperature, food, caffeine, screens, and regularity.

### 3. Stress management

- Chronic stress kills your immune system. Acute stress builds it.
- Exercise is good stress — it makes you stronger.
- Rest and recovery are mandatory, not optional. Take breaks. Stillness restores focus — 15 minutes of nothing, daily.
- Small, steady action beats bursts of intensity.
- Expose yourself to manageable challenges to build resilience.
- Move your body, connect with people, breathe deeply to release stress.
- Physiological sigh: max inhale + extra inhale + slow exhale = instant calm.

### 4. Sunlight

- Get morning sunlight within 1 hour of waking. It sets your body clock.
- 5–25 minutes daily (more on cloudy days).
- Sunset light signals your body to prepare for sleep.

### 5. Hygiene

- Keep your body, hair clean.
- Wash hands before eating and after the bathroom.
- Brush teeth after meals and before sleep. Checkup twice a year.
- Design and Keep your style in clothes.

### 6. Nutrition

- Drink water. Morning and before sleep.
- Eat real food, mostly plants. Avoid processed food.
- Eat protein first — it keeps blood sugar stable.
- Prioritize lean protein: meat, eggs, cottage cheese. Less bread.
- Try intermittent fasting (16:8 or longer) if it suits you.
- To control blood sugar: vegetables first, then protein/fat, then carbs. Walk 10 min after eating.
- No smoking, alcohol, or caffeine. No sugar, fried food, or late-night eating.
- Get vitamins from food. Consider Vitamin D and creatine if needed.
- Use antibiotics only when truly necessary.

### 7. Breathing

- Breathe through your nose. Use your diaphragm.
- Never practice intensive breathing while driving or in water.

### 8. Fitness

Exercise is the single best thing for your mind and body.

- Target weight: height(cm) − 110.
- Move 20–30 minutes daily. Twice daily is ideal.
- If you sweat, breathe hard, and feel your heart — it's working.
- Morning routine: water → exercise/dance → cold shower.
- 5 key exercises: Jefferson curl, reverse Nordic curl, farmer's walk, Cossack squat, weighted pull-up.
- Stand tall, stretch, develop coordination.

### 9. Longevity

- The older you get, the more effort health requires.
- Get regular checkups. Catch problems early.
- Most chronic diseases come from laziness, excess, and short-sightedness.
- Medicine treats. Only you can prevent.
