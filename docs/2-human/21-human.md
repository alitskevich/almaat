# Human Being

**Human Being**: A composition of
biological circuitry, animal drives, social membership, cultural inheritance,
equipped with Intellect and Inspiration.

**Inspiration**: The immanent unstoppable drive and curiosity to push beyond any limits. [[4,5,6,7,46]]

**Human Dignity**: Worth of a Human Being from its Inspiration and innate creative potential.

**Awareness**: Respect for one's own Dignity.

Awareness grants the right — and moreover, the obligation — to fully realize one's Potential.

**Conscience**: Considering each Human Being as a one of the entire Mankind.

Conscience entails

- to recognise the Dignity of each individual in our common movement toward our Bright Future,
where individuals have freely realized their potential, transcending current limitations and achieving greatness.
- to accept the natural right to go their own path and pursue own Goals, as long as they do not infringe on the Dignity of others,
- and to expect comprehensive mutual support/favor/trust from other conscientious people in return for the same.

---

**Absolute Truth**: The absolute transcendent answers to the existentional questions:

*Who am I indeed - Where and why? What is beyond Reality? What next?*

> Unreachable in full, unspoken in words, but the subject of continuous pursuit.

**Own Truth**: One's own relative approximation of Absolute Truth.[[8,9,10,11,12]]

- May be whatever and however you want - idealistic or unconventional, fictional or formal.
- Must not be constrained by external pressure or self-perception, requires no external justification
- Must not be dogmatic — it raises questions, not final answers

**Intellect**: The capacity to develop one's own Truth through one's own discovery.[[1]]

**Core Values**: The set of things reflecting one's Truth - valued more than still alive.

**Faith**: Having an unwavering Commitment to one's Core Values.[[13,14,15,16]]

- Faith is not blind belief — it is a choice to live by one's Truth regardless of circumstances.
- Fills Life with Meaning beyond animal existence
- Breaks the automatism of circumstance (Karma) and enables building oneself beyond what was predetermined
- Faith is the source of courage, resilience, and greatness.

**Happiness**: The perception that one's Life as a whole is meaningful, not futile. [[17]]

> *The measure of Happiness*: readiness to live it through infinite repetition.

---

**Mission / Way / Opus Magnum**: The chosen field of self-realization and behavior guidelines. [[18,19,20]]

- where one's personal Truth, Faith and Inspiration have been applied,
- where one contributes to the global humanistic movement, and thus, fulfills their Life with meaning.

**Wisdom**: The ability to apply one's Truth effectively along the Way.

**Resilience**: The capacity to experience fully — letting life pass through — while remaining oneself, alive and open, in any circumstances.

**Greatness**: Self-respect earned through readiness [[21,22]]

- to make choices and to carry full personal responsibility for them,
- to pay costs, sacrifice without regrets,
- to persevere on the Way with resilience and determination, regardless of emotional state, changed circumstances and unexpected outcomes.

**Maturity**: Understanding and accepting one's emotions; expressing, using, and consciously influencing them.

- Emotions connect to action — acting as someone leads to becoming them
- We choose how we react
- Opposite emotions often manifest similarly — we can name them as we choose
- Congruence is the simplest path to social presence

**Openness**: The position of the eternal student — an unfrozen view of the world, with room for error and growth.[[28,29]]

- Explore with curiosity
- Seek feedback; challenge existing knowledge
- Clear preconceptions and biases

---

**Joy**: transcendent experience of being beyond(not tied to) any Reality - perceiving anything instantly `as-is`:

- Beyond any illusions, self-deception, overthinking, mental frameworks(aware of them, not bound by them)
- without division, attachment, opposition, and finitude, where everything is interwening — each particle contains the Whole, everything changes, similars attracts each others and nothing is precisely predetermined.

> Joy cannot be achieved, deserved or purchased.
> It is not magic nor superpower.
> It is a slight shift in perspective — an obviousness that was always there, waiting to be noticed.

*Joy entails:*

**Acceptance**: Accepting the Present exactly as it is — without evaluation, comparison, resistance, or denial.

It also includes Self-Acceptance: Accepting both one's dark and light sides — without judgment, envy,  but with humor and irony. [[2]]

**Let Past Go**: Releasing what no longer works — without regret, resentment, or despair.

**Moderation**: Contentment with 80% of what is possible. Joy is released when attempts to fix the world generate suffering.

- End relations that share only a distant past
- Learn from mistakes; enjoy memories — but do not cling

**Avoid Expectations**: Renouncing unfulfilled, inflated expectations.
The skill of dreaming about a better future without obsession — ready to accept any outcome calmly.

*Practice*: Recognize your finitude. Ask: What would I do with six months left? Write the epitaph of your current life. Then describe your ideal life.

**Gratitude**: All-encompassing gratitude for everything — toward oneself, parents, children, community, and the universe.

**Forgiveness**: Forgiving oneself and others — holding no grudges, taking nothing personally.

**Asking Forgiveness**: The readiness to ask forgiveness from oneself and others.

---

**Dead-end**: The boundary beyond which a person irreversibly abandons their Dignity.

As long as a person maintains their own attitude, holds even the smallest Goal, and moves toward it — the line has not been crossed.

> [[23,24,25,26,27]]

**Death**: The inevitable end of the physical body, but not of the human being as a whole.

Death is the ultimate test of one's life choices —
it reveals whether one lived with integrity and dignity, or whether they crossed the Dead-end line.

Regardless of the circumstances of death, if one maintained their attitude and commitment to their values until the end,
they have lived a great and happy life.

**Embrace Mortality**: Indifference to being or not being. Death as return to the Nothing from which all begins.
