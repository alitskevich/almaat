# Rajdo

The human's opening into `Rajdo` — perceiving and accepting Being `as-is`, as one interweaving whole — and the `Awareness`, `Acceptance`, and `Love` it entails.

**`Rajdo`** := discovering, perceiving and accepting of Being `as-is`:

- as an entire mental process unfolded from the one single root
- as a unity where everything is interwening - each particle contains the Whole.
- as a dance, where everything changes, splitting and merging,
- as a variations of possibilities, where nothing is predetermined for sure.

---

`Rajdo` Is is always here, it is the ultimate basis of entire human life.

`Rajdo` cannot be deliberately achieved, deserved or purchased. It just happens to become explicit at some time.

`Rajdo` is not magic nor superpower, but a slight shift in perspective - an obviousness that was always there, waiting to be noticed.

`Rajdo` feels made of all others - *"I am because We are"* - and sees others as variants of themselves.

`Rajdo` makes a person vulnerable. Overcoming this fear expands what is possible. [[3]]

---

`Rajdo` entails

**`Awareness`** := conscious alignment with `Rajdo` in every single Moment.

- Beyond any mental illusions, preconcepts, frameworks (aware of them,but not bound by them)
- without strong division to inner and external, dissotiation from Ego, body, mind, emotions.
- without any deceptions, opposition, and finitude

**`Acceptance`** := Accepting the Present exactly as it is - without evaluation, comparison, resistance, or denial.

- have no expectations, daydream about a better future without obsession
- ready to accept any outcome calmly.

**`Self-Acceptance`** := Accepting myself as one of manifestations of God

- in a whole, both dark and light sides;
- track my Ego, do not identify myself with my Ego;
- outside of external validation or confirmation.
- no blame, judgment, envy toward myself, but only humor and irony. [[2]]

**`Moderation`** := Contentment with 80% of what is possible.

- Recognize your finitude. Write the epitaph of your current life.
- End relations that share only a distant past
- Releasing what no longer works - without regret, resentment, or despair.

**`Gratitude`** := All-encompassing thanks, Forgiving and ask forgiveness for everything

- toward oneself, family, every person met, the universe as a whole.
- holding no grudges, taking nothing personally.
- Learn from mistakes; enjoy memories - but do not cling

**`Love`** := Genuine and limitless openness, curiosity, mutual attraction, understanding, compassion, admiration and respect.

---

## Approaching Rajdo

`Rajdo` is given, not built. It tends to surface only *after* long effort and then only when the effort is released — never by force. Practices do not manufacture it; they clear what obscures it.

- **Witness, don't grip.** The first move is to notice that `Thought`s, feelings, and the body are *observed* — they pass through; they are not the observer. This is `Awareness`: aware of the mental stream, not bound by it. No exotic experience is required — the shift is a change of vantage, not a new sensation.
- **Peaks are not it.** Altered and peak states — bliss, dissolution, oneness felt for a few days — come and go, and chasing them is the central trap: each fades and can leave a crash behind. `Rajdo` is not a high but the even, stable ground beneath highs and lows, where everything is lit evenly rather than by a narrow beam. Equanimity, not intensity.
- **The inner critic falls quiet.** What the shift removes is the compulsive, nagging voice of unworthiness — *you are doing the wrong thing, you must achieve, you are defective*. That voice is the interiorized crowd, not you (see [inner speech](../2-mind/12-mind.md#the-social-origin-of-mind)); `Rajdo` is dis-identification from it.

**Traps on the way:**

- **The state that evaporates.** A peak reached at a retreat dissolves on return to ordinary life; treating the peak as the destination sends you chasing the next one forever.
- **The post-peak crash.** Losing a vivid state can drop a person into despair. The state was a glimpse, not a possession; the ground it pointed to does not leave.
- **Fanaticism.** Taking "give up everything" literally, or surrendering judgment to a teacher who "works only with the fearless," turns a `Moderation`-shaped path into self-harm. Fearlessness without discernment is exploitable.
