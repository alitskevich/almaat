**Love** := discovering, perceiving and accepting of Being `as-is`:

- as an entire mental process unfolded from single root
- as a unity where everything is interwening - each particle contains the Whole.
- as a dance, where everything changes, the similars attracts each others and nothing is precisely predetermined.

> Love cannot be deliberately achieved, deserved or purchased.
> It is not magic nor superpower, but a slight shift in perspective - an obviousness that was always there, waiting to be noticed.

Love is the ultimate human need and the final goal of entire human life.

A person in Love feels made of all others - *"I am because We are"* - and sees others as variants of themselves.

Love makes a person vulnerable. Overcoming this fear expands what is possible. [[3]]

Love entails

**`Awareness`** := conscious alignment with high-level vision in every single Moment.

- Beyond any overthinking, mental illusions, preconcepts, frameworks (aware of them,but not bound by them)
- without any deceptions, division, attachment, opposition, and finitude

**`Acceptance`** := Accepting the Present exactly as it is - without evaluation, comparison, resistance, or denial.

- have no expectations, daydream about a better future without obsession
- ready to accept any outcome calmly.

**`Self-Acceptance`** := Accepting myself as one of manifestations of God

- in a whole, both dark and light sides;
- track my Ego, do not identify myself with my Ego;
- outside of external validation or confirmation.
- no blame, judgment, envy toward myself, but only humor and irony. [[2]]

**`Moderation`** := Contentment with 80% of what is possible.

- Recognize your finitude. Write the epitaph of your current life.
- End relations that share only a distant past
- Releasing what no longer works - without regret, resentment, or despair.

**`Gratitude`** := All-encompassing thanks, Forgiving and ask forgiveness for everything

- toward oneself, family, every person met, the universe as a whole.
- holding no grudges, taking nothing personally.
- Learn from mistakes; enjoy memories - but do not cling

**`Openness`** := Genuine and limitless curiosity, mutual attraction, understanding, compassion, respect.
