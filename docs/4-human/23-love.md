**Love** := discovering, perceiving and accepting of Being `as-is`:

- as a whole mental process unfolded from single root - building and percieving own appearance from miriads of different points
- Beyond any overthinking, mental frameworks (aware of them, not bound by them)
- without deceptions, division, attachment, opposition, and finitude, where everything is interwening — each particle contains the Whole, everything changes, similars attracts each others and nothing is precisely predetermined.

> Love cannot be achieved, deserved or purchased.
> It is not magic nor superpower.
> It is a slight shift in perspective — an obviousness that was always there, waiting to be noticed.

Love entails

**`Acceptance`**: Accepting the Present exactly as it is — without evaluation, comparison, resistance, or denial.

**`Self-Acceptance`**: Accepting myself as one of manifestations of God

- in a whole, both dark and light sides;
- track my Ego, do not identify myself with my Ego;
- no blame, judgment, envy toward myself, but only humor and irony. [[2]]

**`Moderation`**: Contentment with 80% of what is possible. Love is released when attempts to fix the world generate suffering.

- End relations that share only a distant past
- Learn from mistakes; enjoy memories — but do not cling

**Avoid Expectations**: Renouncing unfulfilled, inflated expectations.
The skill of dreaming about a better future without obsession — ready to accept any outcome calmly.

**Practice**: Recognize your finitude. Ask: What would I do with six months left? Write the epitaph of your current life. Then describe your ideal life.

**`Gratitude`**: All-encompassing gratitude for everything — toward oneself, parents, children, community, and the universe.

**`Forgiveness`**: Forgiving oneself and others — holding no grudges, taking nothing personally.

**Asking Forgiveness**: The readiness to ask forgiveness from oneself and others.

**Let Past Go**: Releasing what no longer works — without regret, resentment, or despair.

**`Openness`**: Genuine openness and limitless interconnection — mutual attraction, understanding, compassion, respect.

Love is the ultimate human need and the final goal of all social processes.

A person in Love feels made of all others — *"I am because We are"* — and sees others as variants of themselves.

Love makes a person vulnerable. Overcoming this fear expands what is possible. [[3]]
