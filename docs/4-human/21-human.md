# Human Being

**`Human Being`** := composition of biological circuitry, social membership, and cultural inheritance, equipped with [`Intellect`](../2-mind/11-intellect.md) and `Inspiration`.

---

**`Inspiration`** := the drive and curiosity to push beyond any limits. [[4,5,6,7,46]]

---

**`Dignity`** := the worth of a `Human Being` — grounded in adaptability, long-term development, cognitive power, empathy, and the ability to cooperate, but above all in its `Inspiration` and `Intellect` in pursuing [`Truth`](25-truth.md).

---

**`Conscience`** := respect for one's own `Dignity` and for everyone else's, considering each `Human Being` as one of the entire Mankind, which entails:

- to recognise the `Dignity` of each individual in the common movement toward a future in which everyone has realized their potential,
- to accept the natural right to go their own path and pursue their own `Goal`s, as long as they do not infringe on the `Dignity` of others,
- and to expect comprehensive mutual support, favor, and trust from other conscientious people in return for the same.

`Conscience` grants the right, and the obligation, to fully realize one's potential.
