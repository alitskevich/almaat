# Human Being

![Human Being](/images/4-human/21-human.svg)

**`Human Being`** := composition of biological circuitry, social membership, and cultural inheritance, equipped with `Intellect` and `Inspiration`.

---

**`Inspiration`** (Djaga) := the drive and curiosity to push beyond any limits. [[4,5,6,7,46]]

---

**`Dignity`** (Guda) := the worth of a `Human Being` — adaptability, long-term development, cognitive power, empathy, the ability to cooperate, unstoppable creative potential; but most of all, and only, from its `Inspiration` and `Intellect` in pursuing [`Truth`](25-truth.md).

---

**`Conscience`** := respect for one's own `Dignity` and for everyone else's, considering each `Human Being` as one of the entire Mankind, which entails:

- to recognise the `Dignity` of each individual in our common movement toward a Bright Future, where everyone has freely realized their potential, transcending current limitations,
- to accept the natural right to go their own path and pursue their own `Goal`s, as long as they do not infringe on the `Dignity` of others,
- and to expect comprehensive mutual support, favor, and trust from other conscientious people in return for the same.

`Conscience` grants the right — and moreover, the obligation — to fully realize one's potential.
